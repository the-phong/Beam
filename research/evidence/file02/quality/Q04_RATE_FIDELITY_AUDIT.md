**Disposition: PASS for the audited RQ1b strata.** The [locked analysis](../../../protocol/FILE02_Q04_LOCKED_ANALYSIS.md) and tool were committed at `c485297` before generating [`Q04_RATE_FIDELITY.json`](Q04_RATE_FIDELITY.json). `python3 research/tools/file02_q04.py run` and independent `verify` exited 0. The seven A01–A05/A08/A13 and Q02 source sessions reverified frozen raw manifests and their original QC.

The audit covered **248/248 fresh networks and 2,976/2,976 one-second steady windows**, including A13 off/on/off and the four Q02 profiles. For every window it compared planned and measured monotonic **socket-write** counts and encoded bytes separately for total, fixed legitimate and background traffic. All achieved tx/s and byte/s matched the locked targets exactly at one-second resolution: zero windows beyond ±5%, zero below 90%, zero excluded runs. Maximum absolute per-event send-offset jitter was 10.075 ms across the audited set; per-run values and target/achieved windows are in the JSON. A target of zero background tx/s in A13 off windows remained zero.

Offered rate is not confirmed valid throughput. For example, an A01 attack arm offered 241 distinct socket writes, while only 73 valid transactions committed; the other 168 were expected structural rejects. The audit uses `actual_offset_ns` and `wire_bytes` from successful generator socket writes, not accepted mempool count, RPC success count or eventual goodput. The existing RQ1b matched-service inferences need no rate-QC exclusions from this audit. One-second equality does not prove packet-level timing equality, sustainable capacity or network-origin fairness outside the tested loopback RPC topology.
